# Customer posts: Nextdoor and local Facebook groups

Replace **[City]** with the suburb or neighborhood. Use the link with that place's `utm_source` (e.g. `plowbird.app/?promo=FIRSTPLOW&utm_source=nextdoor-richfield`) so signups show which post worked.

**Only post where drivers are signed up.** If someone in a non-pilot area can't order, it costs goodwill.

**Facts used below:** from $35 · pay after it's plowed · live map of the driver · $10 off with FIRSTPLOW (new customers, one per household, expires March 31, 2027) · Storm Pass from $79/mo.

## 2. "Snow emergency tonight" / big storm tonight

Post **when the snow emergency is declared** (Minneapolis and St. Paul usually announce by early evening) or the evening before a 4"+ forecast in the suburbs. Keep it short, since people read these on phones.

### Facebook / Nextdoor (cities with snow emergencies)

> ❄️ **[City] declared a snow emergency tonight.** Plows start on the [Night 1] routes at 9 pm.
>
> If your car's going in the driveway so it doesn't get towed, make sure the driveway is clear. **Plowbird** can send a local plow truck tonight or first thing tomorrow. You'll see the price upfront and pay only after it's plowed.
>
> $10 off your first plow: **FIRSTPLOW** → plowbird.app
>
> (Parking rules for your street: minneapolismn.gov/snow or stpaul.gov/snow.)

**Check the details each time.** Night 1, 2 and 3 rules, start times and links differ between Minneapolis and St. Paul and can change. Copy them from the city's announcement that night rather than from this template.

>
