**Location:** Twin Cities: Richfield, Edina, Bloomington, St. Louis Park, Roseville, Maplewood

---

**Title:**

> Snow plow operators – paid residential driveway jobs, set your own hours

**Body:**

> **Plowbird** is launching on-demand driveway plowing in the Twin Cities this winter. We're signing up 10–15 independent plow operators before the first snow, starting in the first-ring suburbs: Richfield, Edina, Bloomington, St. Louis Park, Roseville and Maplewood.
>
> **How it works**
> 1. A homeowner nearby requests a plow in the app and sees an upfront price.
> 2. You get the job offer with the address, driveway size and pay. Accept it or skip it.
> 3. Plow the driveway and mark it done in the app. The customer is charged only after it's plowed.
> 4. You get paid through Stripe to your bank account after each job.
>
> **Pay**
> - $36 for a standard 2-car driveway. Bigger driveways, sidewalks, deep snow and storm surges pay more.
> - 100% of tips go to you.
> - No lead costs, no marketing, no invoicing, no collections.
>
> **Who this fits**
> - Landscapers and lawn crews who are idle in winter and already own a plow
> - Plow operators with contract routes who want paid work between pushes
> - Contractors with a plow-equipped pickup who want winter income
>
> **Requirements**
> - Truck with a plow in good working order
> - Valid driver's license and a clean driving record
> - **Commercial auto insurance with plow coverage, plus liability insurance** (you'll upload proof)
> - Smartphone (iPhone or Android)
> - Independent contractor: you run your own business and choose your own jobs
>
> **Apply:** plowbird.app/drive
> Approval takes a license and insurance check, truck and plow photos, and a 10-minute phone call. Approved drivers are ready to go when the first 2"+ storm is forecast.
>
> Questions? Reply to this ad.
