# Craigslist: labor gig ad (drivers)

**Where:** minneapolis.craigslist.org › gigs › labor gigs. Choose "I'm offering pay". Repost every 7–10 days through November.
**Location field:** Richfield / Edina / Bloomington (or wherever you most need drivers)

---

**Title (70 characters max):**

> Plow truck owners: $36 per driveway + 100% tips, pick your own jobs

**Body:**

> Have a truck with a plow? Get paid to clear driveways near you this winter.
>
> **Plowbird** is a new Twin Cities app for on-demand driveway plowing, launching in Richfield, Edina, Bloomington, St. Louis Park, Roseville and Maplewood. Homeowners order a plow from their phone. You see the job, the address and the pay before you accept.
>
> - **$36 for a standard 2-car driveway** (bigger driveways, deep snow and storm surges pay more)
> - **You keep 100% of tips**
> - **You choose your jobs.** Go online when you want. Fill gaps between your contract routes, or plow evenings after work.
> - **Paid through Stripe** straight to your bank. No invoices, no chasing customers.
> - Customers pay through the app, so there's no cash to handle.
>
> **You need:**
> - a pickup or truck with a working plow
> - a valid driver's license
> - commercial auto insurance that covers plowing, plus liability insurance
> - a smartphone
>
> **Apply in about 5 minutes:** plowbird.app/drive
> We approve drivers before the first snow. Applications include a quick phone call and photos of your truck and plow.

---

**Notes**
- Add 2–3 photos: the driver poster (`../preview/driver-poster.png`) and an app screenshot of a job offer.
- Craigslist hides contact info behind its relay. Put the link in the body (as above), since many readers won't email.
- To see which ad brings applicants, use `plowbird.app/drive?utm_source=craigslist-gig` if the `/drive` page records `utm_source`.
