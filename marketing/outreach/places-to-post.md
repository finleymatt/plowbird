# Places to post (Twin Cities pilot)

Researched Oct 19, 2026. Post everything yourself. Check each group's rules first: many local groups ban business posts outside a weekly "promo day" or a "recommendations" thread.

Ready-to-post copy is in this folder: `craigslist-*.md`, `facebook-driver-post.md`, `customer-posts.md`.

### Facebook (search these terms while logged in; group names change too often to list)

Logged-out searches don't show Facebook groups, so none are verified here. Search Facebook → Groups for:
- `Minnesota snow plowing`, `MN snow removal`, `Minnesota snow plow subcontractors`, `Twin Cities snow removal`
- `Minnesota landscapers`, `Twin Cities landscaping and lawn care`, `MN lawn care business owners`
- `Minnesota plow trucks for sale`, `MN truck and equipment buy sell`
- Facebook Marketplace: search "snow plow" and "plow truck" within 25 miles. Sellers and buyers of plows are leads.

Pick groups with 1,000+ members and posts in the last week. Ask the admin before posting a recruiting ad if the rules aren't clear. Use `facebook-driver-post.md`.

### Forums

- **PlowSite** (https://www.plowsite.com): the main snow-plowing contractor forum, with regional and "help wanted / subcontractor" threads. Read the rules on recruiting before posting. A helpful reply in a Minnesota thread works better than a cold ad.
- **LawnSite** (https://www.lawnsite.com): the lawn-care sister forum. Winter "what do you do in the off-season" threads are a natural place to mention it.
- **Reddit**: r/landscaping and r/minneapolis. Minneapolis "who plows driveways?" threads appear after big storms, but self-promotion rules are strict, so answer questions and don't post ads.

## Customers

### Nextdoor

- Create a free **Nextdoor Business Page** (business.nextdoor.com) for Plowbird. Nextdoor's guidelines steer business promotion to Business Pages, their posts and paid ads, not the main neighborhood feed. Read the current guidelines before posting. `customer-posts.md` has copy for a Business Page post and for a personal neighbor post.
- The organic win: neighbors ask "who plows driveways?" after a storm. A neighbor who has used Plowbird can recommend it. That beats an ad, so ask early happy customers to do it.
- City and police pages post official snow-emergency updates. Read their posts so you know when to time yours.
