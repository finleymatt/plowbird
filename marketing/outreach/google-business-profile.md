# Google Business Profile

Set up at business.google.com. Needed for "snow plowing near me" searches. Google verifies the business (postcard, phone or video) before the profile goes live, so start a few weeks before the first snow.

## Basics

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Business name | **Plowbird** (just the real name; adding keywords like "Plowbird Snow Removal Minneapolis" breaks Google's rules and gets profiles suspended) |
| Primary category | Snow removal service |
| Additional categories | Type "snow" in the category box and add only ones that are accurate. Don't add landscaping categories you don't offer. |
| Business type | **Service-area business**: hide your address (you don't serve customers at a storefront) |
| Service areas | Richfield, Edina, Bloomington, St. Louis Park, Roseville, Maplewood (add Minneapolis and St. Paul when drivers cover them) |
| Website | https://plowbird.app/?utm_source=google-business |
| Phone | The support number (Google Voice is fine) |
| Hours | Open 24 hours *during winter* or your real support hours. Google lets you set "More hours" for online ordering. |

## Description (750-character limit; this one is about 650)

> Plowbird brings a local plow truck to your driveway on demand. Enter your address at plowbird.app, see an upfront price for your driveway (from $35), and tap Request. No app download needed. Watch your driver arrive on a live map, and pay only after your driveway is plowed. Drivers are local plow operators, and we check their insurance before they can take jobs.
>
> Need it done every time? Storm Pass plows automatically whenever 2" or more of snow is forecast at your address, from $79/month.
>
> Now serving Richfield, Edina, Bloomington, St. Louis Park, Roseville and Maplewood. New customers get $10 off their first plow with code FIRSTPLOW.

*(Google doesn't allow links or promo-heavy text in the description. The code mention is mild, but if the profile gets flagged, delete the last sentence and use an Offer post instead.)*

## Services (add each under "Services")

| Service | Description |
|---|---|
| On-demand driveway plowing | A local plow truck to your driveway, usually within the hour. Upfront price, pay after it's plowed. |
| Storm Pass (automatic plowing) | We plow automatically whenever 2"+ of snow is forecast at your address. From $79/month. |
| Sidewalk and front walk clearing | Add sidewalk clearing to any plow. |
| Scheduled plowing | Book a plow for a set time, e.g. before you leave for work. |

*(Remove "usually within the hour" if acceptance times in the first storms are slower. Only list the sidewalk and scheduled services if they're live in the app; both exist in the code today.)*

## First posts (Updates / Offers)

- **Offer:** "$10 off your first plow": code FIRSTPLOW, ends March 31, 2027, link plowbird.app.
- **Update (storm eve):** "4–6 inches expected tonight. Order from your phone tonight or in the morning, and pay after it's plowed."

## Photos

Logo (the orange snowflake mark), a cover photo from `../assets/hero.html` (the rendered hero image), app screenshots of the live map and price screen, and real photos of driver trucks plowing (with permission) once you have them. Real photos help the profile rank and get clicks.

## Reviews

The gameplan's Phase 3 step: the morning after each storm, ask happy customers for a Google review. Use the "Ask for reviews" share link from the profile. Never offer discounts in exchange for reviews; Google bans it.
